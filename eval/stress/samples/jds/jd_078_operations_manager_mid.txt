Operations Manager
Riverstone Partners · Manchester · Fully on-site · Full-time

About us
Riverstone Partners builds products for the manufacturing plant sector. We are a team of 3000 people with offices in Manchester.

About the role

• Hire, train and coach supervisors
• Own productivity, safety and quality KPIs
• Run daily operations for a site of 50-200 staff
• Lead continuous-improvement projects

Requirements

• operations management with direct reports
• KPI management
• people leadership
• health and safety compliance
• Bachelor's degree preferred

Preferred qualifications
• labour planning
• WMS or ERP systems
• Lean / Six Sigma

Nice to have

• union environment experience
