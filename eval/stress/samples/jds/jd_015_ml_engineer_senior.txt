Senior Machine Learning Engineer
Harbor Health · London · Fully on-site · Full-time

Harbor Health builds products for the recommendations sector. We are a team of 40 people with offices in London.

About the role
As our Senior Machine Learning Engineer, you will fine-tune and serve LLMs and work with product on ML-powered features, working closely with product and engineering.

• Work with product on ML-powered features

• Monitor model drift and performance
• Build feature pipelines
• Train, evaluate and deploy ML models to production

Qualifications
- 6+ years of experience in machine learning
- Python
- machine learning (scikit-learn, XGBoost)
- deep learning (PyTorch or TensorFlow)
- deploying models to production
- Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Statistics or a related quantitative field
- experiment design / A/B testing
- LLM fine-tuning
- MLOps (MLflow, Kubeflow, SageMaker)

Bonus points if you have
- Spark
