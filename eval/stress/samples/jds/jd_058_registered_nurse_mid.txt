Registered Nurse
Solstice Media · Sydney · Hybrid, 3 days in office · Full-time

About us
Solstice Media builds products for the acute care hospital sector. We are a team of 120 people with offices in Sydney.

About the role
As our Registered Nurse, you will administer medications safely and work in a multidisciplinary team, working closely with product and engineering.

What you'll do
• Administer medications safely
• Work in a multidisciplinary team
• Assess, plan and deliver patient care
• Educate patients and families

Qualifications
- 3+ years of experience in nursing
- Registered Nurse license
- acute care clinical experience
- BLS certification
- EHR documentation (Epic or Cerner)
- Diploma or Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN)
- Active Registered Nurse (RN) license in the state (required; applications without it cannot be considered)
- ACLS certification
- ICU or emergency experience

Bonus points if you have
- second language
- BSN degree

Good to know
This role is hybrid, 3 days in office in Sydney; relocation support is available. Please state your notice period when applying.
Up to 30% travel to client sites is expected.

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
