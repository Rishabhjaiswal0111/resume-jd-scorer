Director, Corporate Development
Parallax Games · Pune · Hybrid · Full-time

About us
Parallax Games builds products for the technology M&A sector. We are a team of 40 people with offices in Pune.

About the role
As our Director, Corporate Development, you will run due diligence on transactions and draft pitch books and CIMs, working closely with clients.

What you'll do
• Run due diligence on transactions
• Draft pitch books and CIMs
• Support senior bankers on client meetings
• Build valuation models (DCF, comparables, LBO)

Qualifications
- 8+ years of experience in investment banking, including at least 2 years leading a team or function
- financial modelling and valuation (DCF, LBO)
- M&A or capital markets transaction experience
- Excel and PowerPoint
- accounting knowledge
- Bachelor's in Finance, Economics or similar; MBA for associate level
- client-facing deal experience
- sector expertise
- MBA or CFA

Bonus points if you have
- language skills
- CFA charter

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
