Senior Supply Chain Planner
Kestrel Retail · Bengaluru · Hybrid, 3 days in office · Full-time

About us
Kestrel Retail builds products for the automotive parts sector. We are a team of 3000 people with offices in Bengaluru.

About the role
As our Senior Supply Chain Planner, you will manage supplier orders and lead times and track KPIs such as fill rate and inventory turns, working closely with senior leadership.

What you'll do
• Manage supplier orders and lead times
• Track KPIs such as fill rate and inventory turns
• Find cost savings
• Forecast demand and plan inventory
• Run S&OP inputs

Qualifications
- 6+ years of experience in supply chain planning
- demand planning or inventory management
- Excel (advanced)
- ERP (SAP or Oracle)
- Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain, Operations or Engineering
- supplier management
- S&OP

Bonus points if you have
- Six Sigma

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
