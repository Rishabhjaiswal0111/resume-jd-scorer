Nurse Unit Manager
Saffron Kitchens · New York, NY · Fully on-site · Full-time

About us
Saffron Kitchens builds products for the community clinic sector. We are a team of 600 people with offices in New York, NY.

About the role
As our Nurse Unit Manager, you will administer medications safely and educate patients and families, working closely with senior leadership.

What you'll do
• Administer medications safely
• Educate patients and families
• Document care in the EHR (Epic or Cerner)
• Work rotating 12-hour shifts
• Work in a multidisciplinary team
• Assess, plan and deliver patient care

Qualifications
- 8+ years of experience in nursing, including at least 2 years leading a team or function
- Registered Nurse license
- acute care clinical experience
- BLS certification
- EHR documentation (Epic or Cerner)
- Diploma or Bachelor of Science in Nursing (BSN)
- Active Registered Nurse (RN) license in the state (required; applications without it cannot be considered)
- ICU or emergency experience
- charge nurse experience
- ACLS certification

Bonus points if you have
- second language

Good to know
You must be legally authorised to work in the United States without visa sponsorship.
This role is fully on-site in New York, NY; relocation support is available. Please state your notice period when applying.

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
