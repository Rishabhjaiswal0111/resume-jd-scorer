Customer Support Specialist
Solstice Media · Bengaluru · Hybrid · Full-time

About us
Solstice Media builds products for the telecom sector. We are a team of 40 people with offices in Bengaluru.

About the role
As our Customer Support Specialist, you will write help-centre articles and meet SLA and CSAT targets, working closely with operations.

What you'll do
• Write help-centre articles
• Meet SLA and CSAT targets
• Escalate bugs to engineering with clear reproduction steps
• Resolve customer tickets by chat, email and phone
• Onboard and retain customers

Qualifications
- 3+ years of experience in customer support
- customer support or success experience
- helpdesk tools (Zendesk, Freshdesk or Intercom)
- excellent written communication
- High school diploma required; degree a plus
- knowledge-base writing
- SaaS support

Bonus points if you have
- SQL basics
- second language

Good to know
You must be legally authorised to work in India without visa sponsorship.
Up to 30% travel to client sites is expected.

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
