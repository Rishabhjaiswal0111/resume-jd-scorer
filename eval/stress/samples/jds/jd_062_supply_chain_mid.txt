Supply Chain Analyst
Granite Works · Austin, TX · Remote within the country · Full-time

About us
Granite Works builds products for the FMCG sector. We are a team of 3000 people with offices in Austin, TX.

About the role
As our Supply Chain Analyst, you will forecast demand and plan inventory and track KPIs such as fill rate and inventory turns, working closely with clients.

What you'll do
• Forecast demand and plan inventory
• Track KPIs such as fill rate and inventory turns
• Run S&OP inputs
• Manage supplier orders and lead times
• Work with logistics partners
• Find cost savings

Qualifications
- 3+ years of experience in supply chain planning
- demand planning or inventory management
- Excel (advanced)
- ERP (SAP or Oracle)
- Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain, Operations or Engineering
- SQL or Power BI
- S&OP
- supplier management

Bonus points if you have
- APICS CPIM
- Six Sigma

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
