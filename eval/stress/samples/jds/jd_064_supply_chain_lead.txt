Supply Chain Lead
Helix Robotics · Pune · Hybrid, 3 days in office · Full-time

About us
Helix Robotics builds products for the automotive parts sector. We are a team of 600 people with offices in Pune.

About the role
As our Supply Chain Lead, you will forecast demand and plan inventory and run S&OP inputs, working closely with senior leadership.

What you'll do
• Forecast demand and plan inventory
• Run S&OP inputs
• Work with logistics partners
• Track KPIs such as fill rate and inventory turns
• Find cost savings

Qualifications
- 8+ years of experience in supply chain planning, including at least 2 years leading a team or function
- demand planning or inventory management
- Excel (advanced)
- ERP (SAP or Oracle)
- Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain, Operations or Engineering
- SQL or Power BI
- S&OP

Bonus points if you have
- Six Sigma
- APICS CPIM

Good to know
Up to 30% travel to client sites is expected.

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
