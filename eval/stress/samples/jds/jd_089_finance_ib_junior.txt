Investment Banking Analyst
Everpeak Insurance · London · Remote within the country · Full-time

About us
Everpeak Insurance builds products for the industrials sector. We are a team of 120 people with offices in London.

About the role
As our Investment Banking Analyst, you will build valuation models (DCF, comparables, LBO) and support senior bankers on client meetings, working closely with clients.

What you'll do
• Build valuation models (DCF, comparables, LBO)
• Support senior bankers on client meetings
• Draft pitch books and CIMs
• Manage deal execution timelines

Requirements
• 0-2 years of experience in investment banking
• financial modelling and valuation (DCF, LBO)
• M&A or capital markets transaction experience
• Excel and PowerPoint
• accounting knowledge
• Bachelor's in Finance, Economics or similar; MBA for associate level

Preferred qualifications
• MBA or CFA
• client-facing deal experience
• sector expertise

Nice to have
• CFA charter
• language skills

Good to know
You must be legally authorised to work in the United Kingdom without visa sponsorship.

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
