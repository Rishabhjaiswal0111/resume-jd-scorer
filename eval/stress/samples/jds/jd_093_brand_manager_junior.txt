Assistant Brand Manager
Ironleaf Foods · Sydney · Remote within the country · Full-time

About us
Ironleaf Foods builds products for the personal care sector. We are a team of 600 people with offices in Sydney.

About the role
As our Assistant Brand Manager, you will work with sales on trade promotions and own the brand P&L and annual plan, working closely with senior leadership.

What you'll do
• Work with sales on trade promotions
• Own the brand P&L and annual plan
• Launch new products and line extensions
• Analyse consumer and market-share data (Nielsen)
• Brief and manage creative and media agencies

Qualifications
- 0-2 years of experience in brand management
- brand management in FMCG/CPG
- P&L ownership
- consumer insights and market data (Nielsen, Kantar)
- MBA or equivalent
- MBA preferred
- agency management
- product launches
- trade marketing

Bonus points if you have
- digital / e-commerce marketing

Good to know
You must be legally authorised to work in Australia without visa sponsorship.
