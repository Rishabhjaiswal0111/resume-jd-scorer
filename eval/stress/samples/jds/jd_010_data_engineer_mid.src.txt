Data Engineer
Cedar & Pike · Berlin · Hybrid · Full-time

About us
Cedar & Pike builds products for the telecom sector. We are a team of 40 people with offices in Berlin.

About the role
As our Data Engineer, you will own data quality checks and SLAs and optimise Spark jobs and warehouse cost, working closely with senior leadership.

What you'll do
• Own data quality checks and SLAs
• Optimise Spark jobs and warehouse cost
• Partner with analysts and data scientists
• Orchestrate jobs with Airflow
• Model data in the warehouse for analytics
• Build batch and streaming pipelines

Qualifications
- 3+ years of experience in data engineering
- SQL
- Python
- Apache Spark
- data warehousing (Snowflake, BigQuery or Redshift)
- Bachelor's in Computer Science, Engineering, Statistics or similar
- data modelling (Kimball)
- Airflow
- AWS or GCP

Bonus points if you have
- Terraform
- Databricks
- data governance tooling

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
