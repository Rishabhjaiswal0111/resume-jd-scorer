Teaching Assistant
Juniper Learning · Manchester · Hybrid, 3 days in office · Full-time

About us
Juniper Learning builds products for the IB school sector. We are a team of 3000 people with offices in Manchester.

About the role
As our Teaching Assistant, you will assess students and track progress and contribute to department curriculum, working closely with operations.

What you'll do
• Assess students and track progress
• Contribute to department curriculum
• Communicate with parents
• Differentiate instruction for diverse learners

Requirements

• teaching mathematics at secondary level
• valid teaching certification
• lesson planning and assessment
• Bachelor's degree in Mathematics or Education
• A valid state teaching certificate in Mathematics (required; applications without it cannot be considered)

Preferred qualifications
• IB or AP curriculum
• differentiated instruction

Nice to have
• coaching an extracurricular club

Good to know
You must be legally authorised to work in the United Kingdom without visa sponsorship.
This role is hybrid, 3 days in office in Manchester; relocation support is available. Please state your notice period when applying.
