Operations Supervisor
Northwind Labs · Toronto · Hybrid · Full-time

About us
Northwind Labs builds products for the manufacturing plant sector. We are a team of 3000 people with offices in Toronto.

About the role
As our Operations Supervisor, you will manage shift rosters and labour budgets and own productivity, safety and quality KPIs, working closely with clients.

What you'll do
• Manage shift rosters and labour budgets
• Own productivity, safety and quality KPIs
• Lead continuous-improvement projects
• Hire, train and coach supervisors
• Run daily operations for a site of 50-200 staff

Qualifications
- 0-2 years of experience in operations management
- operations management with direct reports
- KPI management
- people leadership
- health and safety compliance
- Bachelor's degree preferred
- Male candidates preferred for this role
- WMS or ERP systems
- labour planning
- Lean / Six Sigma

Bonus points if you have
- forklift certification

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
