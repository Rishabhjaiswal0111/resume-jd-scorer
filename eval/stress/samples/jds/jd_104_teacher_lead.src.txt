Head of Mathematics Department
Quarry Software · Sydney · Hybrid, 3 days in office · Full-time

About us
Quarry Software builds products for the charter school sector. We are a team of 600 people with offices in Sydney.

About the role
As our Head of Mathematics Department, you will plan and deliver math lessons for grades 9-12 and contribute to department curriculum, working closely with product and engineering.

What you'll do
• Plan and deliver math lessons for grades 9-12
• Contribute to department curriculum
• Assess students and track progress
• Differentiate instruction for diverse learners

Qualifications
- 8+ years of experience in teaching, including at least 2 years leading a team or function
- teaching mathematics at secondary level
- valid teaching certification
- lesson planning and assessment
- Bachelor's degree in Mathematics or Education
- A valid state teaching certificate in Mathematics (required; applications without it cannot be considered)
- differentiated instruction
- IB or AP curriculum

Bonus points if you have
- coaching an extracurricular club

Good to know
This role is hybrid, 3 days in office in Sydney; relocation support is available. Please state your notice period when applying.

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
