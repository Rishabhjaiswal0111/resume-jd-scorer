Product Designer

About us
Nimbus Cloud builds products for the e-commerce sector. We are a team of 3000 people with offices in Singapore.

About the role
As our Product Designer, you will design end-to-end user flows and interfaces and run user research and usability tests, working closely with senior leadership.

What you'll do
• Design end-to-end user flows and interfaces
• Run user research and usability tests
• Work with engineers through delivery
• Prototype interactions

Qualifications
- 3+ years of experience in product design
- product/UX design for digital products
- Figma
- user research and usability testing
- a portfolio showing end-to-end design process
- Degree in design, HCI or equivalent experience
- interaction prototyping
- design systems

Bonus points if you have
- motion design
- front-end coding basics

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
