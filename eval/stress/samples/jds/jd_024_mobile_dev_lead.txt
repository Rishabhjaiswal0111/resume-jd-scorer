Mobile Engineering Lead
Driftwood Travel · Singapore · Hybrid, 3 days in office · Full-time

About the role
As our Mobile Engineering Lead, you will work with backend teams on APIs and write unit and UI tests, working closely with senior leadership.

• Work with backend teams on APIs

• Ship releases through the App Store and Play Store

Requirements

• Swift (iOS) or Kotlin (Android)
• mobile app architecture (MVVM)
• publishing to App Store / Play Store
• Bachelor's degree in a technical field

Preferred qualifications
• offline-first data
• SwiftUI or Jetpack Compose
• CI for mobile (Fastlane)

Nice to have
• accessibility on mobile

Good to know
