Senior Operations Manager
Tidewater Bank · Singapore · Hybrid · Full-time

About us
Tidewater Bank builds products for the last-mile delivery sector. We are a team of 600 people with offices in Singapore.

About the role
As our Senior Operations Manager, you will manage shift rosters and labour budgets and run daily operations for a site of 50-200 staff, working closely with operations.

What you'll do
• Manage shift rosters and labour budgets
• Run daily operations for a site of 50-200 staff
• Lead continuous-improvement projects
• Own productivity, safety and quality KPIs
• Hire, train and coach supervisors

Qualifications
- 6+ years of experience in operations management
- operations management with direct reports
- KPI management
- people leadership
- health and safety compliance
- Bachelor's degree preferred
- WMS or ERP systems
- labour planning
- Lean / Six Sigma

Bonus points if you have
- union environment experience
- forklift certification

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
