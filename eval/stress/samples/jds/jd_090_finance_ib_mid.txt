Investment Banking Associate
Lumen Analytics · London · Remote within the country · Full-time

Lumen Analytics builds products for the industrials sector. We are a team of 120 people with offices in London.

About the role
As our Investment Banking Associate, you will manage deal execution timelines and run due diligence on transactions, working closely with operations.

• Run due diligence on transactions
• Build valuation models (DCF, comparables, LBO)
• Support senior bankers on client meetings
• Draft pitch books and CIMs

Qualifications
- 3+ years of experience in investment banking
- financial modelling and valuation (DCF, LBO)
- M&A or capital markets transaction experience
- Excel and PowerPoint
- accounting knowledge
- Bachelor's in Finance, Economics or similar; MBA for associate level
- client-facing deal experience
- MBA or CFA
- sector expertise

Bonus points if you have
- CFA charter

Good to know
