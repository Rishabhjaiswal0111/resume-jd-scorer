Senior Security Engineer
Maple Grid Energy · Singapore · Fully on-site · Full-time

About us
Maple Grid Energy builds products for the banking sector. We are a team of 600 people with offices in Singapore.

About the role
As our Senior Security Engineer, you will build detection rules in the SIEM and run vulnerability management and penetration tests, working closely with product and engineering.

What you'll do
• Build detection rules in the SIEM
• Run vulnerability management and penetration tests
• Support SOC 2 and ISO 27001 audits
• Respond to security incidents
• Run threat modelling with engineering teams

Requirements
• 6+ years of experience in security engineering
• application or cloud security
• vulnerability assessment and penetration testing
• incident response
• SIEM (Splunk, Sentinel or Elastic)
• Degree in Computer Science, Cybersecurity or equivalent

Preferred qualifications
• SOC 2 / ISO 27001
• Python scripting

Nice to have
• OSCP

Good to know
You must be legally authorised to work in Singapore without visa sponsorship.
