Talent Acquisition Lead
Granite Works · Bengaluru · Hybrid · Full-time

About us
Granite Works builds products for the consulting firm sector. We are a team of 40 people with offices in Bengaluru.

About the role
As our Talent Acquisition Lead, you will partner with hiring managers on role definitions and improve candidate experience, working closely with product and engineering.

What you'll do
• Partner with hiring managers on role definitions
• Improve candidate experience
• Run full-cycle recruiting for assigned roles
• Run offers and negotiations

Qualifications
- 8+ years of experience in talent acquisition, including at least 2 years leading a team or function
- full-cycle recruiting
- sourcing (LinkedIn Recruiter, Boolean search)
- applicant tracking systems (Greenhouse, Lever or Workday)
- Bachelor's degree in Human Resources, Psychology or Business
- employer branding
- technical recruiting
- recruiting metrics and reporting

Bonus points if you have
- SHRM certification
- campus hiring

What we offer
Competitive salary, health cover, learning budget, 25 days leave.
