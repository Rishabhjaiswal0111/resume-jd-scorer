Customer Support Team Lead
Lumen Analytics · London · Fully on-site · Full-time

About us
Lumen Analytics builds products for the online marketplace sector. We are a team of 600 people with offices in London.

About the role
As our Customer Support Team Lead, you will meet SLA and CSAT targets and escalate bugs to engineering with clear reproduction steps, working closely with senior leadership.

What you'll do
• Meet SLA and CSAT targets
• Escalate bugs to engineering with clear reproduction steps
• Onboard and retain customers
• Resolve customer tickets by chat, email and phone

• customer support or success experience
• helpdesk tools (Zendesk, Freshdesk or Intercom)
• excellent written communication
• High school diploma required; degree a plus

Preferred qualifications
• SaaS support
• knowledge-base writing

Nice to have
• SQL basics
• second language
